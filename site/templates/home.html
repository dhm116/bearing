Questions people ask every day

Bearing is for anyone who works on or around software: developers, the person on call, new joiners, team leads, and the AI assistants that now write code alongside them.

  • Who owns this?

    Anyone about to change code they didn't write.

  • Who needs to approve my change?

    A developer opening a change for review.

  • What changed just before it broke?

    The engineer woken up by an alert.

  • Which team is this person on?

    A new joiner finding their way around.

  • What does this team look after?

    A team lead planning work, or checking nothing is left without an owner.

  • Who should my coding assistant ask?

    AI assistants get the same answers people do, so their changes reach the right reviewers.

What makes it different

  • Works with what you have

    Bearing reads the tools you already use. Nobody has to fill in a new catalog or remember to keep it up to date.

  • Shows its sources

    Every answer says where it came from and when, so you can check it instead of taking it on trust.

  • Admits when tools disagree

    If the code and the wiki name different owners, you see both, and can fix the one that's wrong.

  • Open, and yours to shape

    Free and open source, and every part can be swapped for one that suits your organization.
